Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

OctaPDF (operated by XolverLabs) is built with an absolute commitment to privacy, data minimization, and user control.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

OctaPDF is available for anonymous public use without registration. We do not collect names, email addresses, or personal profiles for anonymous sessions. We never sell, monetize, or share your data or documents with third parties.

2. Document Processing & File Deletion

When you upload documents for PDF operations (e.g., merging, splitting, converting, OCR, or watermarking):

  • Your files are transmitted securely over encrypted connections (HTTPS / TLS).
  • Files are processed exclusively in memory or ephemeral temp storage to perform the requested operation.
  • Document contents and user filenames are never permanently stored, inspected, or used for AI or model training.
  • All temporary files are deleted immediately upon completion of the operation. As an automated safety net, a background janitor service reaps any orphaned temporary artifacts older than 30 minutes.

3. Logs and Server Telemetry

Server access and error logs record technical connection metadata (IP addresses, request status codes, and timestamps) solely to maintain service reliability and enforce abuse/rate-limiting protections. System logs have a strict 14-day retention limit and are automatically rotated. User document contents and filenames are never logged at informational levels.

4. Cookies & Tracking

OctaPDF contains zero third-party tracking pixels, zero covert advertising trackers, and zero external surveillance SDKs. Local storage and session cookies are utilized strictly for user-selected preferences (such as dark mode and language choice) and CSRF protection.

5. Contact & Privacy Requests

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our team at privacy@octapdf.com.
